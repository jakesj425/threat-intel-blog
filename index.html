Opinion of a DFIR Guy

TL;DR
  • 19 reports analyzed — 6 Other Campaigns, 4 Nation-State Activity, 3 Credential Theft & Infostealers
  • 11 adversarial groups identified
  • 30 CVEs in active use
  • Ransomware activity across 2 reports
  • Nation-state campaigns in 4 reports
  • Top report: CISA Warns of Active Exploitation of OpenSSH, GPUStack, FreeSWITCH, and IBM Flaw

AI Assisted attacks are heating up. While we can catch them, they move fast. They do a lot of damage in areas of the network that some admins might not even be aware of yet. In some cases, with AI assisted remediation turned on, this wouldn't be a problem, But currently the posture of most networks aren't ready for this level of an attack. And most people don't trust AI to just "do" stuff on their behalf.

At this point the TA trusts their bad AI more than we trust out good AIs, and for no other reason than the good old "UPTIME IS KING" statement we have been fighting since the early 90's

May I suggest a well orchestrated remediation plan, assisted by AI and pre-approved in certain environments. Then if it get's through, (and it will). there is less damage and a much faster investigation, less exposure and a smaller chance of your data leaving before you find the asset it's being taken from.
